## The problem

Most NetSuite accounts accumulate risk quietly. Roles drift until somebody can create and approve their own payments. Admin access piles up. Periods never get closed. Old tokens and dormant logins stay open. None of it shows up until an audit, a bad actor or a renewal invoice forces the issue.

A Health Check catches it in days rather than in a six-week consulting engagement.

## Frequently asked questions

<div class="hc-faq">

### Is it safe? What access do you need?

Read-only access via a scoped, token-based role. The extraction pulls configuration and metadata only, not transaction data. Nothing in your account is modified, and I remove all access objects when the engagement ends.

### How long does it take?

Report within 24 hours. Debrief scheduled with you, usually within the same week.

### What does the score mean?

Each assessed area is rated on its worst finding, and the score is the average of those area ratings out of 100, carrying a letter grade. Any Critical finding caps the grade at D and any High finding caps it at C. Areas that cannot be judged from a read-only extraction are excluded from the score and flagged for manual review rather than counted as clean.

### What if our account is clean?

Then you get documented proof of a well-governed system. Useful for auditors and boards.

### Do you fix the issues too?

Yes. The Health Check is the diagnosis. Remediation is scoped separately, and the fee is credited toward it.

### Does this work for OneWorld / multi-subsidiary?

Yes. The assessment runs account wide, so a OneWorld account is covered in the same pass, and the number of subsidiaries does not change the fee.
