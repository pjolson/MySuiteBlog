## The problem

Most NetSuite accounts accumulate risk silently. Roles drift until someone can create and approve their own payments. Admin access piles up. Periods never get closed. Old tokens and dormant logins stay open. None of it shows up until an audit, a bad actor, or a renewal invoice forces the issue.

A Health Check makes the invisible visible — in 24 hours, not a six-week consulting engagement.

## What we find

<div class="hc-findings-grid">
  <div class="hc-finding">
    <h3>Segregation-of-duties conflicts</h3>
    <p>Users who can both create and approve vendor payments. The classic fraud and audit finding.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hc-finding">
    <h3>Dormant administrator accounts</h3>
    <p>Full admin access nobody's logged into in months.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hc-finding">
    <h3>Open accounting periods</h3>
    <p>Backdated-posting exposure your external auditor lands on first.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hc-finding">
    <h3>Wasted license seats</h3>
    <p>Dormant users sitting on full licenses you're paying for.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hc-finding">
    <h3>Technical debt</h3>
    <p>Legacy SuiteScript 1.0, duplicate vendor records, search and workflow sprawl.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="hc-finding">
    <h3>Security gaps</h3>
    <p>Missing MFA, overprivileged roles, stale integration tokens.</p>
  </div>
</div>
