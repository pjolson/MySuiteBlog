# JRN-08 journal-balance guide: local acceptance

Added JRN-08 locally to the existing numbers-and-spreadsheet-changes guide. The local catalogue now contains 119 explanations across 35 guide pages. No deployment, commit, push, client CSV inspection or NetSuite import was performed. Historical reports retain their historical counts.

## Content and implementation

The general balance explanation starts with one journal's saved amounts, grouping and debit/credit mappings. An explicit two-journal example shows why opposite differences do not cancel for import purposes. The Single Journal Entry workflow is distinguished from multi-journal grouping.

The balanced-file workaround heading and summary are visible outside the expandable general steps. It cites user-supplied SuiteAnswers Answer 70365 and moves complete rows within each journal, preserving all amounts and associations. It does not promise to fix unequal totals or bad mappings. Updates have separate append/replacement/payment-application cautions; intercompany visitors receive their own caution. No sorting, rounding or balancing automation was added.

JRN-07 and its existing anchor remain unchanged, with a reciprocal related link. JRN-05 and numeric/file-grouping guidance are linked. JRN-08 is available under both Transaction lines and Dates and numbers using entry-level category membership; existing entries keep their existing single-category behavior.

The shared phrase matcher recognizes journal entry must balance, including the full error. Natural-language aliases use the existing related-result search, not exact-message rules. The original input is preserved for display.
